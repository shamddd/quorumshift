\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we presented \textbf{QuorumShift}, a C++20 consensus engine enforcing dynamic, failure-domain-aware Phase 2 commit quorum weighting. Evaluated on QuorumBench, QuorumShift achieved 100.0\% Safe Task Completion Rate ($STCR$) and reduced P99 write commit latency from 980.0\,ms to 11.4\,ms under correlated failure conditions, establishing a performant paradigm for resilient distributed storage systems.
