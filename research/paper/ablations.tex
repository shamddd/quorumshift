\section{Ablation & Sensitivity Studies}
\label{sec:ablations}

We ablate individual components of AdaptiveReplica:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{w/o Dynamic Replication Factor}: Disabling $R(t)$ scaling increases latency under heavy write spikes by $3.4\times$.
    \item \textbf{w/o Failure-Domain Placement}: Disabling rack-aware placement drops availability to $96.5\%$ under correlated rack outages.
    \item \textbf{w/o Latency Optimization}: Disabling RTT telemetry integration increases P99 latency from $13.5\,\text{ms}$ to $56.6\,\text{ms}$.
\end{itemize}
