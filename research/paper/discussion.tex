\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

The experimental results validate our hypothesis that continuous telemetry-driven dynamic quorum weighting eliminates tail latency bottlenecks caused by degraded nodes in static majority quorums. By shifting Phase 2 commit quorums dynamically to local healthy nodes during asymmetric latency spikes or correlated rack outages, QuorumShift preserves high write throughput without sacrificing linearizability.
