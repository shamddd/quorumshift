\subsection{Key Empirical Insights}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Availability & Tail Latency Resilience}: AdaptiveReplica achieves **99.97\% availability** and **13.50\,ms P99 latency**, eliminating availability drops seen in static $R=3$ ($95.86\%$) and static $R=5$ ($98.86\%$).
    \item \textbf{Network Traffic Savings}: AdaptiveReplica reduces network replication traffic to $1.15\times$, saving 31\% bandwidth compared to static $R=5$ ($1.67\times$).
    \item \textbf{Linearizability}: Zero stale reads were recorded across all 16 failure scenarios.
\end{enumerate}
