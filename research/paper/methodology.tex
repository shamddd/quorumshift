\section{AdaptiveReplica Design}
\label{sec:methodology}

\subsection{Multi-Signal Policy Engine}
The AdaptiveReplica controller aggregates four real-time signals:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Node Health \& Failure Probability}: Unhealthy node ratio $\alpha = 1 - \frac{|\mathcal{N}_{\text{healthy}}|}{N}$.
    \item \textbf{Network Latency \& Loss}: Exponential moving average of RTT ($\bar{\tau}_i$) and packet loss ($L_i$).
    \item \textbf{Workload Read/Write Ratio}: Dynamic ratio $r_{\text{write}} = \frac{N_{\text{write}}}{N_{\text{total}}}$.
    \item \textbf{Zipfian Access Skew}: Parameterized theta $\theta \in [0.0, 1.5]$.
\end{enumerate}

Replication factor $R(t)$ expands to 5 or 7 under elevated failure or write pressure, and contracts to 3 during steady states.
