\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

We acknowledge the following limitations of this study:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{In-Memory Simulation}: Experiments were conducted in an in-memory C++ cluster simulator. Physical NVMe disk I/O persistence overhead under real hardware conditions remains to be evaluated.
    \item \textbf{Byzantine Resilience}: QuorumShift assumes crash-fault-tolerant (CFT) semantics and does not defend against arbitrary Byzantine node behavior.
\end{enumerate}
