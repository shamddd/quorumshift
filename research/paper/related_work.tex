\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\paragraph{Consensus & Quorum Reconfiguration}
Raft \cite{ongaro2014search} and Multi-Paxos \cite{lamport2001paxos} use static majority quorums. Flexible Paxos \cite{howard2016flexible} demonstrated that Phase 1 election quorums and Phase 2 write quorums need only intersect, allowing asymmetric quorum sizes.

\paragraph{Replica Placement & Elasticity}
CARP \cite{zhang2021carp} introduced correlated failure-aware static placement. Elastic Replication \cite{aguilera2020elastic} scaled replica counts based on workload demand. AdaptiveReplica unifies multi-signal dynamic replication factor adaptation with online topology placement while formally preserving consensus safety.
