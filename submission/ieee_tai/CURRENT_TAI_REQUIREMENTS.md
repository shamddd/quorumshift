# IEEE TAI Submission Requirements & Policy Matrix (`CURRENT_TAI_REQUIREMENTS.md`)

## 1. Requirement Classification Breakdown

### Category A: Confirmed by Supplied Official Template (`TAI_Word_Template.doc`)
1. **Double-Column Format**: Standard IEEE 2-column page layout with 0.5–0.75 in margins.
2. **Abstract Restrictions**: 150–250 words, exactly 1 paragraph, self-contained, no citations, no equations, no footnotes.
3. **Dedicated Impact Statement**: Maximum 150 words, placed immediately after the abstract.
4. **Index Terms**: Alphabetical order, separated by commas, introduced with `Index Terms—`.
5. **Section Numbering**: Roman numerals for main headings (`I. INTRODUCTION`, `II. RELATED WORK`), capital letters for subsections (`A. System Model`).
6. **Equation Numbering**: Consecutive numbering in parentheses `(1)`, flush right.
7. **References**: Numbered IEEE style `[1]`, `[2]` in order of first appearance.

### Category B: Confirmed by Current Official IEEE Author Center & TAI Policies
1. **Generative AI Policy**: Generative AI tools cannot be listed as authors. Material use of AI for code/text editing must be transparently disclosed in Acknowledgments/Title Page.
2. **Double-Anonymous Review**: Main document (PDF/Word) must be fully anonymized. Author details and identifying self-citations must be omitted.
3. **Preprint / Prior Publication**: IEEE permits preprints on arXiv; prior publication in conferences requires $\ge 30\%$ new material and disclosure.
4. **ORCID Identifier**: Recommended for all authors; validated during ScholarOne submission.

### Category C: ScholarOne Upload Interface Requirements
1. **Title Page (Required)**: MS Word format containing manuscript title, all authors' names, affiliations, email addresses, and acknowledgments.
2. **Main Manuscript (Required)**: Anonymized PDF or Word document in IEEE double-column layout.
3. **Cover Letter (Optional / Recommended)**: Brief statement of originality, scope relevance, and confirmation of non-dual submission.

### Category D: Uncertain / Requires Author Verification
1. None. All formatting, anonymization, and upload rules are verified against official IEEE TAI guidelines.
